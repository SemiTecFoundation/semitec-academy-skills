\section{Preparation}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Complete the training \emph{Shift registers} in SemiTec Academy.
  \item Install Vivado and the board files of your board.
  \item Download the lab files: open this lab in SemiTec Academy → \emph{Download folder}.
        You get \texttt{src/rtl}, \texttt{src/sim} and \texttt{src/constraints}.
\end{itemize}
\safetybox{Touch the board only at its edges and discharge yourself (ESD) before handling it. Connect
and disconnect cables only when the board is powered off.}
